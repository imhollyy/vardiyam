- Additive migration v4 creates `break_intervals`, its WorkRecord `ON DELETE CASCADE` FK, stable IDs, UTC minute/date/hour checks, indexes, and overlap/range/WorkRecord-update guard triggers. Existing v1/v2/v3 data is not rewritten; v3→v4 legacy aggregate totals remain without interval rows.

History displays legacy aggregate rows as “30 dk — saati belirtilmemiş”. Timed intervals are only breaks deducted from work duration; no paid-break rule or money calculation.
